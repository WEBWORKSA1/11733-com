Cookie policy

Essential storage

We store your theme choice, whether you dismissed the cookie notice, and whether you've already sent a request (so we don't show prompts again). This uses your browser's local and session storage and is never sent to us.

Advertising cookies

Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies to show and measure ads, including personalized ads. Manage them in Google Ads Settings. Where the law requires consent, Google's consent tools apply.

Embedded content

YouTube videos use youtube-nocookie.com and load only when you press play.

Control

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The calculators keep working without them.
