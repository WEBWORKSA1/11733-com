About 11733 Home Hub

11733 Home Hub is an independent website about one ZIP code: Setauket and East Setauket on Long Island's North Shore. We built it because the biggest financial decisions most families make (selling, buying, renovating) happen locally, while most online tools are national and generic.

What we do

  • Free tools with local defaults: mortgage, affordability, property tax, New York closing costs and rent vs buy.
  • Sourced data: every number names its source and date.
  • Introductions on request: free home value reports, buyer matches and quotes from screened local pros.
  • Community: events, a photo and story contest, and paid work for local creators.

How we make money

Google AdSense display ads, labeled sponsorships, directory listings, and referral or lead fees from professionals you ask us to connect you with. Reader pledges help too. None of these change what we publish or the price you pay. Details are in our disclaimer and editorial policy.

What we're not

We're not a brokerage, lender, law firm or government office. We're not affiliated with the U.S. Postal Service or any local institution named on this site.
