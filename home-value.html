How 11733 homes are valued

Price per square foot is a quick yardstick. Redfin put the 11733 median at $379 per sq ft in October 2025, on a median sale price of $773K. But no two streets in Setauket or East Setauket price alike. Water access, lot size, school assignment, the year of the last renovation and the season all move the number. That's why your report starts from the sales closest to your home in size, age and location, not a county-wide average.
