The 11733 buyer's path in 6 steps

1

Get pre-approved

Sellers here expect it. A full underwriting pre-approval beats a quick pre-qual.

2

Price the whole payment

Suffolk property taxes often add $1,200–$1,600 a month at these prices. Use the tax estimator.

3

Pick an agent and an attorney

In New York, attorneys usually draft and negotiate the contract after an accepted offer.

4

Inspect beyond the house

Ask about cesspool or septic systems, oil tanks, flood zone status and permits for past work.

5

Plan cash to close

At $1M or more, buyers pay New York's 1% "mansion tax". Plan for recording tax and title costs too.

6

Close and claim exemptions

Apply for STAR and any exemptions you qualify for once you own the home.
