Living here

Founded 1655. Home of Washington's spies.

Setauket was the first settlement in what became the Town of Brookhaven, and the hub of the Culper Spy Ring that fed intelligence to George Washington from 1778 to 1783. Today it sits beside Stony Brook University and Stony Brook Medicine, between the harbors of Long Island's North Shore.

What area does ZIP code 11733 cover?

11733 is the postal code for Setauket and East Setauket in the Town of Brookhaven, Suffolk County, New York. Neighboring ZIPs include 11790 (Stony Brook), 11777 (Port Jefferson) and 11794 (Stony Brook University).

Is the home value report really free?

Yes. It costs nothing and carries no obligation. It's an estimate built from recent comparable sales, not an appraisal. If you later want an agent introduction, you choose that.

Is 11733.com a real estate brokerage or lender?

No. We're an independent information site. With your consent, we introduce you to licensed professionals. Some partners may pay us a referral or advertising fee, and that never changes the price you pay. See our disclaimer.

How are pros screened?

Before a pro is listed or matched, we check for an active Suffolk County home improvement license where one is required, proof of insurance and public reviews. You should still check credentials yourself. Our hiring guide shows how.

Where do your numbers come from?

Public sources such as the U.S. Census Bureau's American Community Survey, Freddie Mac, Redfin, Zillow and ATTOM. Every figure on the data page lists its source and date.
