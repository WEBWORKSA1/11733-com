Editorial policy

Sourcing

Every statistic names its source and date. We prefer primary sources: the U.S. Census Bureau, Freddie Mac, government agencies and major market-data providers. Where sources disagree, we say so.

Review

Guides carry a review date. Market figures are refreshed on a schedule, and the site rebuilds automatically every week.

Independence

Advertisers and partners don't review, approve or buy editorial content. Anything sponsored is labeled.

No fake signals

We don't publish invented reviews, testimonials, listings or statistics. Directory slots show as "open" until a real, verified business fills them.

Corrections

Spot an error? Tell us through the contact form. We fix confirmed errors promptly and note significant corrections.

Fair housing

We describe places, amenities and market data. We don't describe or rank areas by the people who live there.
