Selling in 11733 usually starts from a position of strength. Redfin's October 2025 data showed homes here drawing about two offers on average and selling in roughly five weeks, at a median of **$773K**. Zillow reported that about 72% of Setauket sales in mid-2026 closed **above** list price. A strong market still punishes sellers who misprice or skip preparation, though. Here's the playbook.

## 1. Decide your "why" and your deadline

Your timeline drives every other choice. A job move to another state, a downsizing plan or an estate sale each call for a different strategy. If you also need to buy, decide early whether you'll sell first (and rent short-term), buy first with a bridge loan, or ask for a contingency. In a competitive market, buyers rarely accept a sale contingency.

## 2. Know your number before you meet agents

Agents compete for listings, and some "buy" them with optimistic prices. Walk in knowing your range. Start with three to six **closed** sales from the last six months that match your home's size, age, condition and location. Waterfront and water-view homes, lot size and school assignment all move value, so a ZIP-wide median is only a starting point.

- Price per square foot is a sanity check, not a verdict. Redfin's 11733 median was **$379/sq ft** in October 2025.

## 3. Fix what buyers' inspectors will find

The best return comes from boring items: roof leaks, old electrical panels, active water problems and, on Long Island, the **cesspool or septic system**. Suffolk County has many older cesspools, and buyers increasingly ask about them. A pre-listing inspection (usually a few hundred dollars) lets you fix issues on your schedule instead of negotiating them under pressure.

Cosmetic updates that usually pay back include fresh neutral paint, new light fixtures, a power-washed exterior and tidy landscaping. Full kitchen remodels right before a sale rarely return their cost.

## 4. Stage, photograph and launch on a Thursday

Most buyers decide from photos. Professional photography, a floor plan and, for larger homes, a short video tour are standard at 11733 prices. Many agents launch late in the week to build momentum before weekend open houses.

## 5. Understand New York's closing costs

Sellers in New York usually pay:

| Item | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| NYS real estate transfer tax | 0.4% of the sale price |
| Brokerage commission | Negotiable; compare quotes |
| Seller's attorney | Flat fee, often a few thousand dollars |
| Payoff, recording, misc. | Varies |

Run your own numbers in the [closing cost calculator](/closing-costs.html) (switch it to "I'm selling") to see estimated net proceeds.

## 6. Hire an attorney early

In New York, the contract of sale is usually drafted and negotiated by attorneys after a buyer's offer is accepted. Line up a real estate attorney before you list so nothing stalls once an offer arrives.

## 7. Pick the right season

Spring traditionally brings the most buyers, but also the most competing listings. Fall and winter bring fewer buyers who are often more motivated. If your home stands out (renovated, water access, walkable to a village), the season matters less than the price.

## Quick checklist

- Pull your mortgage payoff figure and any HELOC balances
- Gather permits, certificates of occupancy and survey
- Pre-listing inspection, cesspool or septic check included
- Interview at least two agents; compare marketing plans, not just price promises
- Choose a real estate attorney
- Plan your next move and timing
