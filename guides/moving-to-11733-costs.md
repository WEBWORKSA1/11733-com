11733 is one of Long Island's more affluent ZIP codes. The 2023 American Community Survey put the median household income at **$174,931**, more than double the national median. Here's what living here costs, with the math shown.

## Housing: the big three

Take the median sale price of **$773,000** (Redfin, October 2025), with 20% down and the November 19, 2026 average rate of **7.28%**:

| Monthly cost | Amount |
|---|---|
| Principal & interest ($618,400 loan, 30 yrs) | ≈ $4,231 |
| Property tax (2.0% effective, assumed) | ≈ $1,288 |
| Homeowners insurance (assumed $2,400/yr) | ≈ $200 |
| **Total** | **≈ $5,719** |

That's about **$68,600 a year**. Under the common 28% housing guideline, it takes roughly **$245,000** of gross household income, well above the local median. That's why many buyers here bring large down payments or equity from a previous home. Run your own version in the [affordability calculator](/affordability.html).

## Everyday costs

- **Sales tax**: Suffolk County's combined rate is **8.75%**.
- **Transportation**: most households here need at least one car, and many need two. The LIRR's Port Jefferson Branch serves Stony Brook and Port Jefferson for trips to the city.
- **Utilities and heating**: many older homes still heat with oil. Ask for 12 months of heating bills before you buy.
- **Home maintenance**: budget about 1% of the home's value a year for upkeep, more for older or waterfront homes.

## Where the value is

What residents get for the cost: a historic harbor setting, proximity to Stony Brook University and Stony Brook Medicine, the Three Village school district for most addresses, and a strongly owner-occupied community. 85% of homes are owner-occupied.
