Harbor views are part of 11733's appeal, and so is coastal exposure. Whether you're buying or already own, here's how to understand and manage flood and insurance risk.

## 1. Check the flood zone first

Look up any address on FEMA's **Flood Map Service Center**. Zones starting with **A** or **V** are "special flood hazard areas". If you have a federally backed mortgage on a home there, your lender will require flood insurance. Zone **X** means lower risk, not zero risk.

Redfin also shows a First Street flood, fire, wind and heat risk score for 11733 listings. It's a useful second opinion that models future risk, not just today's map.

## 2. Standard homeowners policies don't cover floods

Homeowners insurance generally excludes damage from rising water. Flood coverage comes from the **National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP)** or private insurers. NFIP policies usually have a **30-day waiting period** before coverage starts, except in some situations tied to a new mortgage, so don't wait until a storm is forecast.

## 3. Get an elevation certificate

For homes in or near a flood zone, an **elevation certificate** from a licensed surveyor can lower your flood premium and helps buyers when you sell. Ask the seller whether one exists.

## 4. Wind and storms

Long Island policies often carry a separate **hurricane or windstorm deductible**, usually a percentage of the dwelling coverage rather than a flat dollar amount. Read that line. A 2% deductible on $800,000 of coverage is $16,000 out of pocket.

## 5. Lower your premium

- Bundle home and auto
- Raise your all-other-perils deductible if you have savings
- Install leak sensors, a monitored alarm and storm shutters, then ask about discounts
- Update an old roof. Many insurers price heavily on roof age
- Shop at renewal, using an independent agent who quotes several carriers

## 6. Buyers: price it in before you bid

Ask for insurance quotes **during** your attorney-review period, not after. A high flood premium changes what a home is really worth to you. Add it to the [mortgage calculator](/mortgage.html) as part of your insurance line.

**Want a local insurance agent?** Mention it in your [buyer match](/buyers.html) request.
