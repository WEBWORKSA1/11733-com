**Conclusion:** the only commercially meaningful identity is the **ZIP code**. A hyperlocal site captures "11733 + intent" searches (homes, value, taxes, contractors) in a high-income market where leads are worth the most.

## 2. Concept chosen: **11733 Home Hub**

An independent home, real-estate and local-life hub for 11733, with three lead engines:

1. **Seller leads**: free home-value report (4-step form, live ballpark). Unit economics: a 2.5% listing-side commission on a $773K sale ≈ $19,325. A typical 25–35% referral share ≈ **$4,800–$6,800 per closed referral**, paid only through licensed channels under NY law.
2. **Home-services leads**: 3-step quote form for roofing, HVAC, kitchens, cesspools and more. Sold per lead or as exclusive category sponsorships.
3. **Buyer / lender leads**: buyer match plus lender quotes. Relocation demand comes from the university and hospital.

Plus: AdSense (real estate, mortgage, insurance and home-improvement keywords pay high CPCs), calculator sponsorships, directory listings, newsletter sponsorship, reader pledges and a sponsor-funded contest.

Why not alternatives: a numerology or "11 7 33" angle-number site has low commercial intent and thin AdSense niches. A national tool site can't beat Zillow. Hyperlocal plus calculators plus human-reviewed leads is defensible.
