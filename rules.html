Official contest rules

1. Sponsor

The North Shore Photo & Story Contest ("Contest") is run by 11733.com ("we"). It is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Instagram, YouTube, Google or any other platform.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States who are 18 or older when they enter. Our team members and their immediate families may not enter. Void where prohibited.

3. No purchase necessary

Entry is free. A purchase or donation does not improve your chance of winning. This is a game of skill, not chance.

4. Entry period

Each round's entry dates are posted on the contest page before the round opens. Entries received outside those dates are not judged. Limit one entry per person per category per round.

5. How to enter

Submit the entry form with a working link to your original work. Entries must be your own work, must not infringe anyone's rights and must not contain unlawful, hateful, sexual or dangerous content. You must have permission from anyone identifiable in your entry.

6. Judging

A panel of at least three judges scores eligible entries on creativity (40%), technical skill (30%) and local relevance (30%). Judges' decisions are final.

7. Prizes

The prize pool is funded by sponsors and supporters. The amount awarded per category is posted on the contest page before judging begins. If no eligible entries are received in a category, its share may roll over. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Prizes may require a signed declaration of eligibility.

8. Rights

You keep ownership of your entry. By entering, you give 11733.com a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display your entry, with credit, on 11733.com and its social channels for contest promotion.

9. Privacy

We use entrant data only to run the contest, as described in our privacy policy.

10. General

We may cancel or change the contest if fraud, technical failure or events outside our control affect it. New York law governs these rules. Questions: use our contact form.
