Living in Setauket & East Setauket

A historic harbor community on Long Island's North Shore, next to one of New York's largest research universities. Here's what moving here looks like.

Where 11733 is

ZIP code 11733 covers Setauket and East Setauket in the Town of Brookhaven, Suffolk County. It sits roughly halfway between Manhattan and Montauk, about 60 miles from each. Neighbors include the villages of Old Field, Poquott and Port Jefferson, plus the hamlet of Stony Brook. Together, Setauket, Stony Brook and Old Field are known locally as the "Three Village" area. Since the 2020 census, the Census Bureau has counted Setauket and East Setauket as two separate places, though most residents still treat them as one community.

A short history

1655: Setauket is founded, the first settlement in what became the Town of Brookhaven.
1778–1783: The Culper Spy Ring, led locally by Abraham Woodhull ("Samuel Culper"), passes intelligence from British-held New York to George Washington.
1800s: Shipbuilding and industry grow around East Setauket's harbor.
1939 onward: Philanthropist Ward Melville reshapes the area, building the Stony Brook Village Center.
1962: Melville's land gift brings the state university to Stony Brook. Its campus borders the Setaukets.

Work & anchors

Stony Brook University and Brookhaven National Laboratory together account for about half the employees of the Town of Brookhaven's ten largest employers. Stony Brook University Hospital is the largest academic medical center on Long Island. That steady base of faculty, clinicians, researchers and graduate students shapes housing demand, especially for homes close to campus.

Outdoors & culture

  • Harbors and beaches: Setauket Harbor and Port Jefferson Harbor open onto Long Island Sound. West Meadow Beach is next door in Stony Brook.
  • History: The Three Village Historical Society runs the "SPIES!" exhibit and Culper Spy Day. The Setauket Village Green is shared by two historic churches.
  • Village life: Stony Brook Village Center, the Stony Brook Grist Mill and the Long Island Museum are a short drive away, as are Port Jefferson's restaurants and ferry.

Getting around

Most residents drive. The Long Island Rail Road's Port Jefferson Branch stops at Stony Brook and Port Jefferson. The Port Jefferson ferry crosses the Sound to Bridgeport, Connecticut. Long Island MacArthur Airport in Islip is about 30 minutes away by car, and JFK and LaGuardia are about 1 to 1.5 hours.

Housing at a glance

The housing stock is mostly owner-occupied single-family homes, from mid-century ranches and colonials to waterfront estates. The median sale price was about $773K in October 2025, with homes typically selling in about five weeks.
