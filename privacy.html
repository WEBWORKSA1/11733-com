Privacy policy

Last updated {{ site.data.build.updated }}

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms (for example your name, email, phone, property address and project details) and standard technical data such as browser type, pages viewed and approximate location from your IP address. Our calculators run in your browser, and the numbers you enter are not sent to us.

How we use it

To answer your request, prepare the report or match you asked for, and, only with your consent, introduce you to the professionals you chose. We may send the newsletter if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time. We don't sell personal information to data brokers.

Sharing

We share your request only with the professionals you choose, and with service providers that help run the site. If the law requires it, we will disclose information.

Form processing

Form submissions reach our private inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service. Its privacy terms apply to that delivery.

Advertising & cookies

We use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads to you based on those visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Videos load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain only after you press play.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California under the CCPA/CPRA, the EU and UK under the GDPR, or Canada under PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to its use. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Retention & security

We keep lead and contact data for up to 24 months unless you ask us to delete it sooner, and job applications for up to 12 months. We use HTTPS and limit who can access the data.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We'll post any changes on this page with a new date.
